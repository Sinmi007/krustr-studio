The pastor says John 3:16. You type jn 3 16 and press Space. The verse is on the livestream before anyone has turned the page.

What it does

Everything Sunday needs on screen.

One window for the Bible, the songs and the cameras. Preview what's next, then take it live with one key.

Bible

Find any verse as fast as it's read.

Type a reference the way people say it. Krustr finds it, puts it in Preview and steps through the passage verse by verse.

  • Short references like jn 3 16, 1 cor 13 4-7 or ps 23
  • English versions, plus Yorùbá, Igbo and Hausa Bibles
  • Next verse with Page Down, live as you step

Sermon

Hear the verse. Show the verse.

Krustr listens to the preacher and writes out the sermon as he speaks. When he names a passage, it appears as a card, and one click puts it in Preview.

  • A live transcript, sentence by sentence, from the sound desk or a microphone
  • Catches "Romans chapter 3 verse 23" and "now look at verse 18" as they're said
  • Nothing goes on air until you take it

Live

Preview first. Take it live when it's right.

Line up the next graphic while the current one is on air. Space plays the animation, Enter cuts. Lower thirds, full screens and cameras each sit on their own layer.

  • Lower third, full screen and camera layers, each with Hide
  • A browser-source address for OBS and vMix
  • A projector output for a second screen

Layouts

Camera and verse side by side, like a conference stage.

Build a layout once: a camera box, a content box, your logo and a strip for the event name. Every verse or lyric you pick fills the content box.

  • Your church's colours, fonts and logo on every graphic
  • One camera feed reused across layouts
  • Switch arrangements between services in a click

Get started

Ready before the first hymn.

  1. 1

    Install it on the media laptop

    Download for Windows and run the installer. No account to create.

  2. 2

    Add your church's look

    Name, logo, colours and fonts go on every graphic, including what's already on air.

  3. 3

    Connect and go live

    Paste the output address into OBS or vMix as a browser source, or open the projector on a second screen.
