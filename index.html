Bible

Find any verse as fast as it's read

Type a reference the way people say it. Krustr finds it, puts it in Preview, and steps through the passage verse by verse.

  • Short references like jn 3 16, 1 cor 13 4-7 or ps 23
  • Many English versions, plus Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa Bibles online
  • Next verse with Page Down, live as you step

Live

Preview first. Take it live when it's right.

Line up the next graphic while the current one is on air. Space plays the animation, Enter cuts. Lower thirds, full screens and cameras sit on their own layers.

  • Lower third, full screen and input layers, each with Hide
  • A browser-source URL for OBS and vMix
  • A second-screen projector output

Layouts

Camera and verse side by side, like a conference broadcast

Build a layout once: a camera box, a content box, your logo and a strip for the event name. Every verse or lyric you pick fills the content box.

  • Your church's colours, fonts and logo on every graphic
  • Reuse a camera feed across layouts
  • Change the arrangement between services, not during them

  1. Install it on the media laptop

    Download for Windows and run the installer. No account, no setup wizard.
